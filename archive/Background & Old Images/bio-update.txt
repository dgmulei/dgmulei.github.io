David Mulei is a screenwriter, playwright, and producer whose work spans television, theater, and film. He has collaborated with Emmy-winning directors, Grammy-nominated musicians, and Broadway actors.

His television credits include story editor on *Veronica Mars* (Kristen Bell) and staff positions on CBS dramas featuring Christopher Lloyd, Judy Greer, and Mare Winningham. His stage work—including full-length plays *The Crowded Hour*, *Stubborn Things*, and *Household Effects*—has been developed at the Sheen Center for Thought & Culture, Royal Family Performing Arts, Open Fist Theatre, and Rogue Machine Theatre. He has worked extensively with award-winning stage and film director Maureen Payne-Hahner, founding member of The Gift Theatre and artistic director of Writers Innovative Network.

Recent projects include an adaptation of *Errands*, a novel by Judith Guest (Ordinary People, Academy Award Best Picture); the libretto for *The Mystic Rose*, a commissioned oratorio with New England Conservatory composer Felicia Sandler that premiered at Boston's historic Basilica; and *Here Is a Man*, an award-winning short film he co-developed with actor/writer Jarvis Matthews. David's non-fiction book *Our Game* was accepted into the Baseball Hall of Fame's permanent collection at Cooperstown. He has taught scriptwriting at Massachusetts College of Art and Design.
