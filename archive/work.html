The Mystic Rose

sacred oratorio

A sacred oratorio drawing from the writings of four female saints. Working in collaboration with composer Felicia Sandler, the libretto mines centuries of spiritual testimony, translating contemplative text into dramatic architecture suitable for sacred performance.

Collaborators: Composer Felicia Sandler

World Premiere: The Basilica of Our Lady of Perpetual Help

Here Is a Man

short film · 2023

A dramatic portrait of soul legend Donny Hathaway. Co-developed with actor and writer Jarvis D. Matthews, the film translates the singer's life and artistry into a 19-minute narrative directed by Sideeq Heard.

Director: Sideeq Heard

Co-writer: Jarvis D. Matthews

Festivals: Official Selection, People's Film Festival (2024); Official Selection, Katra Film Series (2023)

Errands

television miniseries pilot

Adapted from Judith Guest's novel, Errands. Guest's third novel following Ordinary People (adapted into the 1981 Academy Award Best Picture). The adaptation preserves Guest's intimate character work while constructing narrative architecture for serialized television.

Co-writer: Rudi Schwab (Emmy- and Cannes Lion-winning director)

Painted Porch Audio Series

audio drama series

A scripted audio series built for headphones and close listening. The collection includes adaptations of classical sources—Hero & Leander, Tolstoy—alongside original dramatic work. Produced in collaboration with director Maureen Payne-Hahner and featuring performers including Ken Baltin, Jarvis D. Matthews, Jamie Geiger, and Kim Park.

Role: Creator, Writer, Producer

Director: Maureen Payne-Hahner

The Crowded Hour

full-length play

A drama exploring Theodore Roosevelt in the aftermath of his son Quentin's death in World War I. Months in the archives—presidential papers, personal correspondence, war records—compressed into a portrait of grief colliding with political ambition. The play locates dramatic tension in the gap between Roosevelt's public mythology and private devastation.

Workshops & Readings: Manhattan Theatre Club (2016); Beachwood Living Room Series (2015)

Director: Maureen Payne-Hahner

Stubborn Things

full-length play

A historical drama incorporating trial transcripts from Irving v. Lipstadt, the landmark libel case about Holocaust denial. The play mines legal testimony and historical record to dramatize the collision between scholarship and ideology, evidence and belief. The source material—thousands of pages of trial transcript—demanded a dramaturgical approach that honored factual accuracy while constructing theatrical momentum.

Workshops & Readings: Royal Family Performing Arts, Times Square (2019); Beachwood Living Room Series (2016, 2013); Arena Stage at Theatre of Arts (2014)

Director: Maureen Payne-Hahner

Household Effects

full-length play

A domestic drama set within the foreign service. The play explores the tension between diplomatic ambition and married life, between professional duty and personal desire. Research included interviews with foreign service officers and immersion in State Department protocols and culture.

Workshops & Readings: The Road Theatre Co (2019); Open Fist @ The Matrix Theatre, First Look Festival (2015); Rogue Machine (2013); Upright Citizens Brigade (2012)

Directors: Neil H. Weiss, Martha Demson

Our Game: From Unknowns to Tarp Skunks

book · 2020

155 years (and counting) of baseball in Jamestown, New York. A narrative history constructed from local newspaper archives, oral histories, and community records, tracing the evolution of small-town baseball from amateur leagues to professional affiliation.

Publisher: Chautauqua Sports Hall of Fame / Jamestown Tarp Skunks
