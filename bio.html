Bio

David Mulei is a writer and dramaturg whose work spans theater, television, film, and long-form narrative. His practice is rooted in research: archival investigation, historical analysis, and close reading of source material.

He has written for television series including Veronica Mars (The CW), Love Monkey (CBS), and Clubhouse (CBS). His theatrical work has been developed at Manhattan Theatre Club, Royal Family Performing Arts, The Road Theatre Company, and Open Fist Theatre.

His book, Our Game: From Unknowns to Tarp Skunks, chronicles 155 years of baseball history in Jamestown, New York.

Education

  • MS, Graduate School of Journalism, Columbia University
  • BA with Honors, American Studies, Wesleyan University

Teaching

David teaches scriptwriting at Massachusetts College of Art and Design, where he developed the course "Scriptwriting: Crafting the Dramatic Scene," a writing-intensive workshop designed to cultivate the student's distinctive voice.

He has also served as guest instructor at Boston Latin School, offering condensed scriptwriting workshops in collaboration with the BLS Writes student group.

Fellowships & Professional Affiliation
