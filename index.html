Bio

David Mulei is a screenwriter, playwright, and producer whose work spans television, theater, and film.

His television credits include story editor on Veronica Mars starring Kristen Bell and staff positions on CBS dramas featuring Judy Greer, Christopher Lloyd, and Mare Winningham. His stage work—including full-length plays The Crowded Hour, Stubborn Things, and Household Effects—has been developed at the Sheen Center for Thought & Culture, Royal Family Performing Arts, Open Fist Theatre, and Rogue Machine Theatre.

Recent projects include The Mystic Rose, a commissioned oratorio with New England Conservatory faculty composer Felicia Sandler that premiered at Boston's historic Basilica; Here Is a Man, an award-winning short film co-developed with actor/writer Jarvis Matthews; and the pilot script adaptation of Errands, a novel by Judith Guest (Ordinary People, Academy Award Best Picture). David's non-fiction book Our Game was accepted into the Baseball Hall of Fame's permanent collection in Cooperstown, NY. He has taught scriptwriting at Massachusetts College of Art and Design.

Early in his career, David also taught high-school afterschool enrichment in Los Angeles and directed R&D for Sylvan Learning test prep. He is currently collaborating on Smarter Premed with fellow Wesleyan alum Jude Hoffner, who brings two decades of product development experience to the project. As the father of a premed daughter, David sees a field dominated by rankings, forum threads and blogs, promotional content, and private coaching that's out of reach for most families. Smarter Premed is an alternative: a fact-checked guide that gives premeds and their advisors the tools they need to take on what may be the most consequential and least transparent admissions process in higher education.
